# Sales notes: six open deals (invented)

**Everything in this file is invented** except the totals. The brief says six open deals mention automatic sorting, worth £2.3M a year between them, and that one is the biggest deal in the pipeline. The split, the prospects and every quote are made up. Prospect codes are placeholders.

Notes are written in the account executive's voice, as they'd appear in the CRM. They are the rep's reading of the prospect, not the prospect's own words, unless quoted.

| Deal | Value a year | Prospect |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | £720k | Large multi-line insurer, about 1,100 handlers. Biggest deal in the pipeline. |
| D2 | £480k | Direct-to-consumer motor insurer, app-based. |
| D3 | £390k | Regional insurer, motor and home. |
| D4 | £310k | Mid-size insurer replacing an in-house system. |
| D5 | £250k | Specialist home insurer. |
| D6 | £150k | Small managing general agent. |
| **Total** | **£2,300k** | |

## D1, £720k, RFP stage, decision due January 2027

RFP requirement 14: "AI-assisted triage, with an explainable reason recorded for every decision." Procurement asked in the Q&A round how decision records would be shown to their regulator. They saw the competitor's demo in August.

Rep: "This is a must-have. They won't shortlist us without it. Every other requirement we're fine on."

## D2, £480k, demo done, proposal sent

They promise customers payment within 24 hours on simple motor claims and miss it on about a fifth of claims (their figure, from the demo call). Their claims director said: "We want claims sorted the second they land."

Rep: "Automatic sorting is the headline ask. I think what they actually care about is the 24-hour promise."

## D3, £390k, discovery

The ops director wants to cut handler headcount by 15% over two years. Automatic sorting came up as "how we get there".

Rep: "They're a cost play. If we can show handler hours saved, the sorting question goes away."

## D4, £310k, shortlisted

They ticked "automatic sorting" as required on a 60-item requirements checklist. It didn't come up in either demo.

Rep: "Honestly not sure it matters to them. But it's a red cell on the scoresheet."

## D5, £250k, proposal stage

They asked whether we can sort the easy claims in bulk. When I said bulk sorting ships in about six weeks, they were positive, but the head of claims said: "Bulk is nice. Automatic is what the board asked about."

Rep: "Bulk might be enough for the users. Not sure it's enough for the buyer."

## D6, £150k, early stage

They raised the competitor's pricing first, then automatic sorting. About 40 handlers.

Rep: "Price-led. Sorting feels like a reason to say no rather than a reason to say yes."
