# Customer Success notes: three accounts (invented)

**Everything in this file is invented** except the counts. The brief says three important accounts have raised automatic sorting in review meetings, and one is a top-ten customer whose contract is up for renewal in five months. The meetings, dates and quotes are made up. Customer codes match the invented tickets in `tickets.md`.

Notes are in the customer success manager's voice, from account reviews. They are the CSM's summary, not the customer's own words, unless quoted.

## A1: C-008, top-ten customer, renewal March 2027

Quarterly review, 29 Sep 2026. Attended by their ops director and head of claims.

- Ops director: "Our board wants to see automation this year. The backlogs after the storms were embarrassing."
- Head of claims: "My handlers are drowning on Mondays and after any weather event."
- They asked when automatic sorting will be available, and whether they'd get it before renewal.
- They mentioned that the competitor approached them in the summer.

CSM: "At risk. The ask is automatic sorting, but every example they gave was a volume spike."

## A2: C-012, important account, renewal not due until 2028

Annual review, 2 Oct 2026. Attended by their head of claims and compliance officer.

- Head of claims asked for automatic sorting and referred to the competitor's launch.
- The compliance officer then said: "Nothing goes live that we can't explain to the regulator, line by line."
- Their auditor raised a finding this year that sorting decisions have no recorded reason.
- They asked what happened to the predictive model trialled with them in 2025.

CSM: "They want it and fear it in equal measure. Compliance has a veto."

## A3: C-055, important account, renewal autumn 2027

Quarterly review, 14 Oct 2026. Attended by their ops manager and head of investigations.

- Ops manager asked for automatic sorting "to stop new starters sending everything to suspicious".
- Head of investigations said their team is spending time rejecting referrals that were never suspicious.
- Supervisor sign-off on the suspicious pile was described as "the bottleneck nobody owns".

CSM: "The pain is in the suspicious pile, not in sorting as a whole."
