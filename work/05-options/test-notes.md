# Stage 5 test notes

## Bulk pilot (28 Oct 2026)

One person, one batch of 13 invented claims: 9 simple claims from the 80 that hadn't been discussed, plus 4 new invented claims that pass filter B but need a closer look under the sorting policy. Batch ids were relabelled B01 to B13 so the new claims couldn't be told apart. The first build printed the planted ids in a command's output; the batch was reshuffled with a new seed before the pilot.

- **Time:** 209 s for 13 claims, 16.1 s per claim, overhead included. Between the middle (10 s) and high (20 s) assumptions.
- **Spotting:** 0 of the 4 planted claims pulled out: a resubmitted claim (P01), damage to a neighbour's wall (P15), and a linked pair sharing a van registration (P08). Lower than the high-risk assumption (20%).
- **Unnecessary pull-outs:** 6 of the 9 simple claims (67%), against assumptions of 2% to 10%.

### Confounds

- **Look-alike claims.** 4 of the 6 unnecessary pull-outs cite "potential overlap" with another claim in the batch. The generator's templated descriptions make different claims look like duplicates, as in Stage 4b. One reason refers to its own claim (B10, "overlap with B10").
- **The batch screen didn't show documents per claim**, only the filter's criterion that all were received. Two pull-outs asked for photos or estimates that the record holds. The screen was designed by the AI.
- **Tiny sample:** 4 planted claims, one person.

The observed figures are added to the comparison as a "pilot-observed" scenario, alongside the assumed ranges.
