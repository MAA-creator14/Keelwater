# Stage 5 decision

Decided by the human at the Stage 5 gate on 28 Oct 2026.

## Option taken forward

1. **Bulk sorting ships as planned** (about six weeks), with three fixes from the pilots: documents shown for each claim, a similar-claims check, and a reason recorded for every batch.
2. **The A1 timeline study runs at the same time** on about 80 real claims from the last 12 months. Each claim's time from arrival to sorting is split into queue, waiting for information, waiting for approval and handler work, against the thresholds locked at Stage 3.
3. **Discovery on AI suggestions continues** with Sales and design-partner customers, run by the PM and designer. No engineering time and no delivery date. Compliance reviews the reason format during design. Suggestions stay human-decided (the AI only flags claims for a handler), and any new design must pass A3a and A3b on real claims with real handlers before it goes beyond discovery. Iterate once A1 reports.

Auto-clear stays ruled out.

## Gate weights for later stages

Stage 6 spec: standard. Stage 7 build: light. Stage 8 release: formal (the `SKILL.md` defaults).

## Reversal condition

A1 shows the queue is 15% or less of the four-hour time. That moves the main effort to the information and approval flow and keeps suggestions in discovery.
